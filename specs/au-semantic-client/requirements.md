# AU-SEMANTIC-001 requirements

| ID | Requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R001` | **OntologyLifecycle.set_active() fails closed, not a silent local fallback.** `OntologyLifecycle.set_active()` must not silently instantiate a local fallback implementation in place of the EG-served ontology lifecycle method; when that served method cannot be reached it fails closed instead of masking the condition. | A fault test forces the EG ontology-lifecycle method to be unavailable and asserts `set_active()` raises rather than returning a local fallback instance. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R002` | **AU verifies domain-pack class names against EG's schema-class list.** AU resolves every domain-pack class and property reference through EG's served graph-schema-classes read and refuses to use a reference to an unknown class rather than only flagging it as unverified. | A negative test that references an unknown class in a domain pack asserts AU refuses it instead of merely recording it as unverified. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R003` | **AU's knowledge_graph shape files are published to the EG pack or removed.** Every SHACL shape file under `knowledge_graph/shapes/` is either published into the EG-validated schema pack so EG applies it during validation, or deleted from AU together with the tests that reference it; AU holds no SHACL shape outside a published pack. | The previously skipped shape tests run and pass against EG-served validation, or are deleted along with the removed shape file. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R004` | **AU's remaining SHACL shape files move into EG-owned schema packs.** AU's `knowledge_graph/shapes/*.ttl` files and `ontology/shapes/governance.shapes.ttl` are removed from AU and hosted only in EG-owned schema packs or sources; AU references each shape by IRI and performs no local SHACL parsing. | A forbidden-file census confirms the shape files are absent from AU and shape references resolve only through the EG-served contract. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R005` | **Ontology lifecycle, integrity and schema parsing become EG client calls.** `knowledge_graph/ontology/lifecycle.py`, `ontology_integrity.py`, the pack/schema loaders (`domain_packs/pack_loader.py`, `models/schema_pack_loader.py`) and `extraction/extraction_schema.py` no longer parse or hash RDF/OWL/RDFS locally; each becomes a typed call to the generated EG ontology-inspection client. | Generated-client conformance tests and a forbidden-import census confirm no local graph parsing remains in these modules. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R006` | **AU removes rdflib and submits typed payloads to EG instead.** AU's runtime modules that build RDF documents for validation, including the harness, workflow, SHACL, connector-certification, connector-manifest and team-sharing gates, submit typed request payloads to the generated EG client instead of constructing RDF graphs; the `rdflib` dependency and its extras are removed from AU. | `tests/gates/test_epistemic_graph_schema_authority_cutover.py` statically forbids `rdflib`, `pyshacl`, `owlrl` and `owlready2` imports across AU runtime and test code. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R007` | **AU tests drop pyshacl and assert through EG shape validation.** AU test files no longer import `pyshacl` or use an `importorskip('pyshacl')` guard that silently skips the assertion; every shape assertion runs against the EG-served `shacl_validate_ad_hoc` method or a committed validation path. | A forbidden-import census over AU tests fails on any `pyshacl` import, and the affected tests execute, rather than skip, against served EG validation. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R008` | **Reasoning-topology selection uses a served policy, not a local outcome store.** `agent_utilities/graph/reasoning/topology.py` selects a reasoning-graph topology, such as chain-of-thought or tree-of-thought, through a served, calibrated decision policy with explicit abstention rather than from its own self-reported outcome store; the local exponential-moving-average outcome store is deleted. | `check_topology_authority.py` is extended to fail if topology selection reads from a local outcome store instead of the served decision policy. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R009` | **The local engine facade is deleted in favor of the generated EG client.** AU removes its local engine facade and orchestration modules, including `kg/core/engine.py` and its task, ingestion, memory, query, resolver, transport, lock and breaker companions, `kg/facade.py`, and `kg/orchestration/engine_*.py`, and reaches the equivalent functionality only through the generated EG client. Split producer-first: this requirement depends on EG's not-yet-shipped `runnable_skill_derivation` op (tracked by `EG-REPO-INGEST-R002`'s `skill_workflow_ingest` capability); see `AU-SEMANTIC-R009.1`/`.2`. | A caller-inventory and import census confirm every public entry point that used the deleted facade now resolves through the generated client with equivalent authorization and error semantics. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R009.1` | **A typed `runnable_skill_derivation` client contract fails closed while EG's op is absent.** `api/skill_derivation_client.py` declares the typed request/response shape and `RunnableSkillDerivationClient` Protocol AU will call once EG's generated `SourceIngest` client exposes `runnable_skill_derivation` (producer: `EG-REPO-INGEST-R002`), and `resolve_runnable_skill_derivation_client()` raises `RunnableSkillDerivationUnavailableError` rather than deriving locally or returning a default while that op is absent. | A test confirms `resolve_runnable_skill_derivation_client()` raises with EG's generated client absent or lacking the op, and that the Protocol/dataclasses have the declared shape. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R009.2` | **AU's engine-facade callers consume the shipped `runnable_skill_derivation` op.** Once `EG-REPO-INGEST-R002` ships `runnable_skill_derivation` on the generated `SourceIngest` client, `resolve_runnable_skill_derivation_client()` is wired to the real call, and the local engine-facade modules this requirement names are deleted, closing `AU-SEMANTIC-R009`. | A caller-inventory and import census confirm every public entry point that used the deleted facade now resolves through the generated client with equivalent authorization and error semantics. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R010` | **Graph-compute and session facades route through the generated EG client.** AU's graph-compute and session modules, including `kg/core/graph_compute.py`, `session.py`, `epistemic_row.py`, `ogm.py`, the `company_brain*` modules, and `core/registry/kg_adapter.py`, are replaced by calls into the generated EG client, with AU keeping exactly one typed composition of that client under `api/`. | Caller-parity tests confirm callers of the removed facades now use the single `api/` client composition with equivalent behavior. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R010.1` | **AU holds one typed composition of the generated EG client for graph-compute/session ops.** AU defines `GraphComputeClient`/`GeneratedGraphComputeSurface` under `api/graph_compute_client.py` as the single typed model callers will compose against for the surface `kg/core/graph_compute.py`, `session.py`, `epistemic_row.py`, `ogm.py`, the `company_brain*` modules, and `core/registry/kg_adapter.py` currently serve locally; it holds no local graph-compute/session logic of its own. | A negative test confirms construction fails closed with `GraphComputeClientUnavailable` when the installed EG client is `None` or does not implement the required surface, and succeeds and delegates when it does. |
| `AU-SEMANTIC-R011` | **Durable jobs, queues and state move to EG.** AU's durable-work modules, including `kg/core/work_durability.py` and its queue-backend, scheduler, drain, ingest-routing, event and bitemporal companions, its ingest/analytics worker entry points, `core/shared_resource_leases.py`, and `core/state_store.py`/`chat_persistence.py`, are replaced by calls to EG's served durable-job, capacity-lease and state methods; AU holds no durable queue or state authority of its own. | A restart/fault test confirms durable work and state survive through EG rather than a local store, and the removed worker scripts are absent. |
